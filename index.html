How Mo works

  1. Answer a few questions

    Tell Mo where it aches and how much time you have. It takes about a minute.

  2. Get your path

    Mo builds a learning path around your neck, back or jaw, one level at a time.

  3. Move 5 minutes a day

    Follow Mo move by move with a timer. Keep your streak and watch your pain check-ins improve.

Why a few minutes of exercise is worth it

The World Health Organization's 2023 guideline on chronic low back pain recommends exercise programmes as one of its non-surgical treatments.

WHO guideline for non-surgical management of chronic primary low back pain in adults, 2023

A 2021 Cochrane review found that exercise therapy probably reduces pain and improves function in chronic low back pain, compared with no treatment or usual care.

Hayden et al., Exercise therapy for chronic low back pain, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2021

These sources are about exercise in general, not about Mo specifically. Mo is a wellness app, not a treatment.

Questions, answered

Is Mo free?

You can start free. Free covers 5-minute guided routines, the learning path, streaks and your pain check-ins, with a daily energy limit. Mo Pro removes the limit and adds voice coaching, SOS relief sessions, longer routines and more.

How does the free trial work?

The weekly Pro plan ($6.99 per week) starts with a 3-day free trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription starts and renews each week. The annual plan is $39.99 per year. Prices vary by country and are shown in your local currency in the app.

How do I cancel on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Mo and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or billing period ends.

How do I cancel on Android?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions and Subscriptions. Choose Mo and tap Cancel subscription.

Is Mo medical advice?

No. Mo is a general wellness app with gentle exercises. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If your pain gets worse, or you have numbness, weakness, fever or pain after an injury, please see a doctor.

What happens to my data?

Your data stays on your device. Your routines, streaks and pain check-ins are stored locally on your phone. Read the full details in the privacy policy.

Where can I download Mo?

Mo is available now on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.
