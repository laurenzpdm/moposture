The short version

Your exercise history, pain check-ins, goals and settings are stored only on your device. Posture scan photos are analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework and are never uploaded. We have no user accounts, no ads, no analytics SDK and no tracking. We do not sell data and do not use your data to train AI.

Android version

The Android version of Mo is distributed through Google Play. It processes the same on-device data as described above, with these differences: it has no posture scan and no Ask Mo chat. Like the iPhone app, it sends anonymous usage counts (event name, app version and app language, without any user or device ID) to our own server in Frankfurt, as described in the table above. Purchase and subscription status with an anonymous app user ID is sent to Google Play and RevenueCat to provide and restore Mo Pro. Creator codes: to check a creator code, the app downloads the list of valid codes from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub, Inc., USA, which receives the technical connection data of the request such as the IP address). If you open Mo through a creator's link, the app reads the Google Play install referrer once on your device to recognise the code. When you use a valid code, the code is stored with your anonymous RevenueCat user ID so that we can pay the creator their share of the revenue; legal basis is our legitimate interest in paying creators correctly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

What we do not do

No advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of data, no health data sent to servers except the pain context you choose to share in Ask Mo, no AI training on your data.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Because your app data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time with "Reset data" or by deleting the app. You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und für das Recht auf Akteneinsicht Brandenburg.
