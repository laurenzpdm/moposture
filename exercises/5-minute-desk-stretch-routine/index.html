General information, not medical advice. Move gently and stop if anything hurts. You need a sturdy chair, so avoid office chairs that roll or tip for the chair back opener.

The routine, step by step

Prefer to read? Here is every move. In the timer each one gets 5 seconds to get ready, and the last 10 seconds are for slow breathing.

1. Shoulder Rolls (30 seconds)

  1. Stand or sit tall with your arms relaxed.
  2. Lift your shoulders toward your ears, then roll them back and down in a big circle.
  3. After half the reps, reverse the direction.

Dose: 8 slow reps, 4 in each direction.

Tip: Keep your jaw soft and breathe out as your shoulders drop.

2. Chin Tuck (40 seconds)

  1. Sit or stand tall and look straight ahead.
  2. Glide your chin straight back, as if making a double chin. Keep your eyes level.
  3. Hold for 3 seconds, then relax.

Dose: 8 reps.

Tip: Move slowly. You should feel a light stretch at the base of your skull, never pain.

3. Neck Side Stretch (50 seconds)

  1. Sit tall and let both shoulders drop away from your ears.
  2. Tilt your right ear toward your right shoulder until you feel a gentle stretch.
  3. Breathe slowly, then switch sides.

Dose: 20 seconds per side.

Tip: Do not pull on your head. Let its weight do the work.

4. Shoulder Blade Squeeze (50 seconds)

  1. Sit or stand tall with your arms relaxed and palms facing forward.
  2. Draw your shoulder blades back and down, as if tucking them into your back pockets.
  3. Hold, then release. Repeat 8 times without shrugging.

Dose: 8 holds of about 5 seconds.

Tip: Think of a gentle squeeze, not a hard pinch.

5. Chair Back Opener (45 seconds)

  1. Sit on a sturdy chair with your upper back against the top of the backrest.
  2. Clasp your hands behind your head to support your neck.
  3. Gently lean back over the backrest, then come up.

Dose: 6 reps.

Tip: Lean only as far as feels good. Keep your ribs from flaring.

6. Seated Spinal Twist (45 seconds)

  1. Sit tall near the front of a chair with your feet flat.
  2. Cross your arms over your chest and rotate gently to the right, keeping your hips still.
  3. Hold for 3 seconds, return, then rotate to the left.

Dose: 6 reps.

Tip: Turn from your ribcage, not your neck. Keep both sit bones on the seat.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing to keep up with the clock. Doing fewer reps slowly is fine.
  • Pulling on your head during the neck stretch. Let its weight do the work.
  • Holding your breath. Breathe slowly throughout.
  • Pushing into pain. Reduce the range or stop.

When to stop and see a professional

Stop and speak to a doctor or physiotherapist, or seek emergency care where noted, if you notice:

  • numbness, tingling or weakness in an arm, hand or leg
  • pain that spreads (radiates) down an arm or leg
  • pain that started after an accident, fall or other injury
  • chest pain, shortness of breath or pain with sweating (seek emergency care)
  • pain that keeps getting worse, wakes you at night, or comes with fever or unexplained weight loss
