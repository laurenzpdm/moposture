Support

Questions, ideas or a bug? Write to thuemmleraiprojects@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Restore purchases

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Mo Posture and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Reminders do not arrive

Check Settings > Notifications > Mo Posture on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed and Focus modes do not block them.

Delete my data

All your data lives on your device. Use Settings > Reset data in the app, or delete the app.
